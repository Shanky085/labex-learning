# LabEx Learning History

A structured record of completed LabEx hands-on labs. Completion dates and lab names should be taken from the LabEx activity history rather than reconstructed from memory.

## How to use this log

For each completed lab, record:

- lab number
- exact LabEx lab title
- learning area
- completion date (when available from history)
- one-line technical takeaway
- link to notes or practice code, when available
